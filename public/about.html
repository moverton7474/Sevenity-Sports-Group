About & Contact

Chase Clemmons

Chase Clemmons is the founder of Sevenity Sports Group, an athlete development and advisory firm focused on performance and strategy. He's a former Kennesaw State collegiate athlete and Sports Management graduate who built Sevenity around the thing he could not find as a player: one person responsible for the whole picture.

He has been the athlete trying to hold a trainer, a content creator, and a recruiting timeline together at the same time — and he knows which of those actually moves a career forward. Sevenity works across high school, college, transfer-portal, and emerging athletes, with player development, positioning, and brand strategy handled by one team instead of three.

Develop the athlete. Build the brand. Create the opportunity.
