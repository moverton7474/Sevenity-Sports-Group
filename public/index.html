What We Are

One firm for the whole picture.

Sevenity Sports Group brings on-court training, personal development plans, athlete consulting and advisory, and content and personal branding together under one roof — backed by a nationwide coaching and scouting network. An athlete's training, opportunities, and brand shouldn't be handled by three different people who never talk to each other. At Sevenity, they're handled by one.

From high school and college athletes to those navigating the transfer portal, Sevenity builds the plan, does the work, and stays in the room for what comes next.

The Founder

The Founder.

Chase Clemmons

Chase Clemmons is the founder of Sevenity Sports Group, an athlete development and advisory firm focused on performance and strategy. He's a former Kennesaw State collegiate athlete and Sports Management graduate who built Sevenity around the thing he could not find as a player: one person responsible for the whole picture.

He has been the athlete trying to hold a trainer, a content creator, and a recruiting timeline together at the same time — and he knows which of those actually moves a career forward. Sevenity works across high school, college, transfer-portal, and emerging athletes, with training, positioning, and brand strategy handled by one team instead of three.
